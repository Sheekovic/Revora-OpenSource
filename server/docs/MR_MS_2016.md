## Beauty/Wise Revora tier (2026-09-30)

Combined female places9-30 receive permanent Beauty20222022; corresponding male
places receive permanent Wise20252025. Existing vote limits and UID tie-breaks

Goddess/Handsome, and Orchid/Tulip flora rules remain unchanged.

The schema migration defaults existing seasons to false, preserving their saved
terms. Closed seasons are never reopened. New tiers use the existing atomic
judging, saved-award recovery and permanent wardrobe grants. Recovery refuses
a Beauty/Wise row belonging to a season without the new tier flag.

uses supplied profiles, NPC proximity and calendar time, not seeded standings or

earned old-season migration fixture was not run. The existing small-title fixture

## Local voting and frozen awards (2026-09-22)

Ordinary characters register as `mr` (body 1003/1004) or `ms` (2001/2002)
through Twin City guild envoy 380, which also records orchid/tulip votes and
shows standings. Flower choice opens a paged candidate menu (options 226-233)
with the displayed character numbers held in short-lived player state so a later
registration cannot redirect the click. More loads the next uid page; Previous
restores the pinned page that was shown, including after an intervening
registration. A menu opened in one Pacific month cannot vote after that month
ends, even before the coordinator freezes standings.
Each voter may cast one orchid vote and one tulip vote per contest per season.
Self-votes are rejected. Recasting the same flower category is rejected.

A Pacific month calendar freezes the elapsed season at midnight on the first
of the next month. Register, vote and freeze share one season lock so a ballot
cannot land after standings are snapshotted. Vote totals break ties by lowest character number
(`row_number` ordered by votes descending, then uid). Flora awards have one
winner per flower and contest. Combined places two through eight award at most
seven Goddess or Handsome titles per contest.

Freeze commits:

Equal flora votes resolve to one winner by lowest character number. Combined
rank 1 does not also receive the 2nd-8th title. Awards use `mrms-2016-revora-v1`
with source key `season:title`.
Acknowledgement is atomic with the grant. Replay of a frozen season does not
rewrite standings.
